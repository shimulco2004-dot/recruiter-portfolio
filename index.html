Graphic Designer & Generative AI Visual Creator.

I’m MD Shimul, a Bangladeshi creative based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. I’ve been working in graphic design since 2022, creating brand identities, social media designs and commercial graphics with a focus on clear communication, thoughtful layouts and a consistent visual style.

Generative AI is another part of my creative workflow. I develop AI-generated images, concept visuals and video content for brands and campaigns, combining creative direction with design skills to shape the final result.

My hands-on print-production background supports this creative work. I prepare clean vector artwork, large-format graphics, DTF/UV DTF designs and laser-ready cut files—bringing practical knowledge of materials, sizing and output requirements to the design process.
