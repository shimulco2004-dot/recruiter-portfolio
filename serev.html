02 / PROJECT OVERVIEWTHE FICTIONAL BRIEF

Form, in motion.

SEREV is a fictional contemporary fashion atelier exploring fluid layers and quiet structure. The Between introduces its imagined first collection through an intimate Riyadh presentation, a tactile showroom and an editorial campaign for stylists, editors and creative buyers.

03 / THE CHALLENGE

How can an event identity hold attention while leaving the clothes in the foreground? It must feel expressive on paper, legible in a gallery and calm enough to frame a moving silhouette.

04 / THE IDEA

The seam between two moments.

One off-center seam organizes the identity. It holds an expressive serif title against open space, echoing the meeting of fabric panels. The campaign finds its emotion in an unfinished gesture, a falling sleeve and the transition between poses.

12 / PROJECT SUMMARY

An event identity that lets fashion lead: editorial art direction, tactile print, restrained environmental graphics and a consistent digital campaign, supported by practical material and scale decisions.

Self-Initiated Concept Project

SEREV, its collection and the event are fictional. No client commission, attendance, commercial result or delivered event is claimed. Mockups are AI-assisted concept visualizations; SVGs provide the original flat artwork.

Materials and dimensions communicate design intent. These are concept masters, not fabrication-approved files. A venue survey, engineered supports, fire-rating checks, font preparation, print proofs and supplier sign-off would precede production.
