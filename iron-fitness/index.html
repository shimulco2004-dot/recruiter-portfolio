01 / The ideaIRON FITNESS / Controlled Energy

Strength should improve life.

IRON FITNESS connects the discipline of training with the freedom to participate more fully in life. The system is designed primarily for women, with a confident and supportive point of view.

03 / Existing identityOriginal logo preserved

Preserved.

06 / Member experienceClarity at every step

Your first
chapter.

Cards, trainer IDs, workout notebook, assessment sheets and a welcome kit turn the first visit into a considered experience.

Each object has a role: orient, record, carry or support.

07 / Environment and productsUseful at human scale

Find your
next move.

Zone numbers, written destinations and directional arrows help people move through the gym. Matte materials and selective lime improve legibility.

IRON FUEL and IRON HYDRATE create a clear product structure. Product information, claims and manufacturing specifications remain controlled placeholders pending verification.

08 / Digital experienceA clear next action

Your next step,
in your hand.

The digital concept includes an eight-page website and eleven mobile screens for orientation, sessions, demo booking, progress, membership and check-in.

Prototype interactions are illustrative only: no live payments, account access or membership data are connected.

10 / Project contextDesigner-directed concept work

Designed.
Directed.
Refined.

This is a proposed identity expansion around the designer's existing client logo. The brand system, environments, products and digital experiences are concept work.

The project avoids invented commercial claims, product certifications and fake operational information. Its editable identity system provides the design reference for future production development.
