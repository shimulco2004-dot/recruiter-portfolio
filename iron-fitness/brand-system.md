# IRON FITNESS - Brand system and production notes

Completed concept development across Phases 02-13. The user authorised continuous execution without intermediate approval gates.

## Brand foundation

**Purpose** - Help women build strength and confidence to participate more fully in everyday life.

**Platform** - STRONGER FOR LIFE. links training to personal capability. CONTROLLED ENERGY translates that thought into design.

**Application test** - Every design should help someone understand, choose, navigate, participate or recognise the brand.

## Logo master

**Master asset** - The supplied SVG contains the original paths. logo-source.svg is preserved byte-for-byte in the package.

**Application variants** - logo-white.svg uses white plus the original lime on a dark field. logo-black.svg provides the dark application. logo-original.svg preserves source colours.

**Source clean-up** - An off-artboard embedded raster was excluded from application exports. The visible logo paths were not redrawn.

## Clear space and size

**Clear space** - Use at least one wordmark cap-height around the full lockup. In the supplied vector, this is approximately 26.6 source units.

**Minimum size** - Proposed starting points: full lockup 40 mm in print or 160 px on screen; IF symbol 8 mm or 32 px. Test the actual substrate and viewing distance.

**Optical placement** - Keep clear space intact while shifting the complete lockup slightly where its shape needs optical balance.

## Logo restrictions

**Do** - Use the approved artwork; preserve aspect ratio; choose a background with adequate contrast.

**Avoid** - Do not stretch, rotate, redraw, outline, distort or separately rearrange the symbol and wordmark. Do not replace the wordmark with live type.

**Mockups** - Photographic renders communicate intent. Use the supplied vector masters for production, because generated render lettering is not an exact manufacturing reference.

## Colour palette

**Core** - Iron Black #101210; White #FFFFFF; Energy Lime #BAD532; Paper #F3F4ED.

**Source record** - Original SVG dark fill: #142615. Original accent: #BAD532. The dark source colour remains archived; the brief directs a black-and-white application system.

**Colour production** - RGB and hexadecimal are digital references. Convert through the printer's specified ICC profile and approve a physical proof; no unverified spot-colour match is claimed.

## Colour behaviour

**Rhythm** - Aim for roughly 70% neutral foundation, 20% high contrast and 10% lime across a sequence, not as a rigid ratio on each object.

**Readable pairings** - Use dark text on lime and white text on black. Avoid white body text on lime. Data and wayfinding also need written labels.

**Information states** - Lime signals action or emphasis. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning or booking state.

## Typography

**Display** - Impact: short statements, campaign lines and large numbers. Do not imitate the custom wordmark.

**Information** - Arial regular and bold: navigation, headings, body and technical copy. Courier New: compact labels and metrics.

**Font handling** - Live SVG text uses installed fonts. Supply properly licensed fonts or outline final approved text before production. Font files are not redistributed in this package.

## Type scale

**Web** - Body 16-20 px, supporting labels 14 px where practical, display 58-158 px by viewport. Keep essential copy distinct from small metadata.

**Print** - Begin body copy near 9-11 pt for handheld material; proof at actual size. Packaging legal text must follow applicable requirements.

**Optical corrections** - Review kerning in display lines, line breaks and punctuation. Avoid widows and mechanically identical headline sizes.

## Grid and spacing

**Layout** - Twelve columns for wide layouts; four on mobile. Use 5% outer margins on the case-study surface and visible gutters.

**Rhythm** - Alternate hero, quiet statement, detail and information. Limit each panel to one main idea.

**Exceptions** - A controlled crop or oversized headline may break the grid if readability and hierarchy improve. Avoid accidental near-alignments.

## Energy Bars

**Construction** - Three horizontal cut bars derived from the lime wordmark geometry. The application artwork uses a stepped sequence.

**Usage** - Use one cluster in a focal area. Leave enough quiet space to read the three-part rhythm.

**Limits** - The bars are graphic language, not a substitute logo. A progress indicator must correspond to actual data and include a text label.

## Photography

**Subjects** - Adult women participating in strength, cardio, mobility, recovery or community moments. Respect individual proportions and natural expression.

**Materials and light** - Keep skin texture, folds, subtle sweat, surface grain and plausible shadows. Mix environmental, close and documentary viewpoints.

**Inspection** - Review anatomy, hands, equipment, mirrors, logos, text and reflections. Select or correct visuals before delivery.

## Voice and tone

**Voice** - Use familiar verbs, useful next steps and concise sentences. Keep the member's own goals central.

**Examples** - Invitation: Start with the strength you have. Return: Welcome back. Let's find your next step. Platform: STRONGER FOR LIFE.

**Boundaries** - Avoid shame, guaranteed results, invented testimonials, unsupported claims and fabricated offers.

## Member materials

**Cards and IDs** - Front and reverse faces are provided for cards. IDs reserve room for a verified name and portrait; sample IDs are not valid for entry.

**Paper system** - Letterhead, assessment conversation sheet, workout cover and interior, and welcome card share a clear hierarchy.

**Production** - Start with 3 mm bleed and 4 mm safe margins on cards, subject to supplier template. Uncoated paper supports writing and tactile use.

## Welcome kit

**Contents** - Card, notebook, towel, bottle, resistance band and snack concept. The insert plan separates useful objects clearly.

**Structure** - Proposed rigid box with recyclable paper-based insert. Final dimensions depend on the selected products and packing tolerances.

**Validation** - Build a physical fit sample, test lid clearance and transit movement, and confirm food-contact packaging requirements for the snack.

## Environmental branding

**Hierarchy** - Destination first, zone number second, brand signature where useful. The sign family covers 16 environmental touchpoints.

**Placement** - Confirm real room names, sightlines, lighting, height and access routes on a site survey. The artwork is not an architectural or safety plan.

**Fabrication** - Use matte surfaces where reflections reduce reading. Confirm mounting, fire ratings, floor slip resistance and local accessibility requirements with suppliers.

## Apparel and accessories

**Placement** - Small IF marks at chest or hem; selective bars at cuffs, calf or ankle; larger statements on occasional pieces.

**Technical checks** - Test embroidery density, stretch inks, seam clearance, wash fastness and contrast at the proposed scale.

**Function** - Keep bottle measurement areas, mat grip zones and garment stretch points clear. Placement masters are not sewing patterns.

## Product architecture

**Master brand** - IRON FITNESS endorses all extensions. IRON FUEL covers protein and snacks. IRON HYDRATE covers drink concepts.

**Names** - CORE / Vanilla, POWER / Chocolate and RECOVER / Strawberry form the proposed protein trio.

**Resolved ambiguity** - RECOVER is a product name within IRON FUEL. A separate IRON RECOVER family is deferred. Commercial availability and naming checks remain necessary.

## Packaging hierarchy

**Front** - Brand, range, product type, variant/flavour and verified quantity. Keep legal information distinct from marketing copy.

**Back** - Ingredients, allergens, nutrition, preparation, storage, manufacturer, batch and expiry. All depend on verified product data.

**Codes** - Dedicated areas are reserved for authorised barcode and QR artwork. No invented machine-readable code is included.

## Packaging production

**Geometry** - Use the supplier's dieline. Provisional seam reserve is 8 mm and safe area 5 mm; these are starting points, not approved manufacturing dimensions.

**Materials** - Consider matte film wrinkles, tub curvature, can print limitations, opening zones and label adhesion.

**Release conditions** - Proof actual artwork on the selected material. Verify formulation copy, net quantity, claims and required language before sale.

## Social system

**Coverage** - Twelve themes: brand, membership, training, trainer, fitness education, health education, schedule, community, motivation, progress, campaign and promotion.

**Formats** - 4:5 posts, 9:16 story and reel cover, three carousel pages, five highlight covers and a landscape digital ad.

**Crop rules** - Keep important story copy in the central safe region. Review actual platform overlays and current export requirements before publishing.

## Digital experience

**Website** - Home, About, Membership, Classes, Trainers, Wellness, Shop and Contact. Navigation and demo actions work across mobile and desktop.

**Mobile** - Eleven screens cover the member journey. Data, booking and membership content are explicitly illustrative.

**Interaction** - Local demo bookings and notes can be saved on this device. Live authentication, payment and secure access require a future connected implementation.

## Campaign

**Platform** - STRONGER FOR LIFE. stays central. Selected supporting lines include MOVE STRONG. and BUILT FOR YOUR LIFE.

**Formats** - Hero and supporting posters, billboard, mall face, screen, window, web hero and social campaign face.

**Art direction** - Connect a training moment with a recognisable everyday ambition. Avoid implying guaranteed results or inventing member success stories.

## Design decisions

**Direction considered** - A lime-dominant identity was set aside because it made the accent constant. A rigid centred layout was set aside because it repeated the same visual rhythm.

**Selected direction** - Neutral editorial space, strong black/white contrast and selective lime carry Controlled Energy. Large statements alternate with quiet information.

**Refinements made** - Preserved original paths; separated mockups from production masters; resolved RECOVER naming; added a short-sleeve performance view; varied application composition and scale.

## Handoff and quality

**Included** - Case study, guidelines, strategy, 96 SVG application masters, asset manifest, concept photography, eight-page website and eleven-screen mobile prototype.

**Source of record** - Editable SVGs and structured copy govern future production. Photo mockups show intent. Brand source SVG remains preserved.

**Before manufacture or launch** - Confirm the real business details, product formulations, translations, supplier dimensions and final proofs. No fabricated commercial outcome is claimed.

## Fidelity and release status

This is a complete concept-design package. Generated photography may interpret small print and logo details; exact supplied vector masters are provided separately for production. QR and barcode areas intentionally contain labelled placeholders. Business addresses, staff names, prices, product data and legal copy were not supplied and have not been invented. Manufacturing files require supplier dielines and approval. The digital experiences are working presentation prototypes, not deployed business operations.
