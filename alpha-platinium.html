02 / The challengeALPHA PLATINIUM

Make the journey
feel considered.

A real-estate brand must hold together across very different scales: a small access card, a detailed document, a shared space and a large building sign.

The challenge was to retain the supplied mark while creating a visual language that gives each touchpoint a clear role.

04 / The supplied identityALPHA PLATINIUM

One mark.
A wider world.

The symbol, bilingual lettering and original spelling are retained. Horizontal and stacked lockups provide flexibility. Navy, black and reversed assets keep production straightforward.

Logo geometry is preserved in the supplied vector assets. Generated mockups illustrate material and placement; the vectors remain the production reference.
