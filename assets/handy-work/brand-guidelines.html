02 / Logo

The supplied master SVG is the authority. Preserve geometry, proportions, lettering and descriptor. The logo spells HANDY WORKS; do not replace its lettering with the project title. Symbol-only use is permitted in compact contexts.

Clear space: x = width of one outer upright. Keep at least x around the complete logo. Complete logo recommended minimum 45 mm / 220 px; symbol 8 mm / 32 px. Confirm descriptor legibility on the actual material.

Approved versions: navy, white, black and charcoal. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, change individual letters or recolor with unapproved colors.

05 / Graphic system

Use disciplined grids, fine technical rules, modular frames and a single orange finishing corner. The low-contrast pattern repeats the approved existing symbol. Preserve space around typography.

06 / Applications

Business cards and formal documents use exact artwork. Fleet graphics use white paint, navy lower vinyl and small orange corners. Keep glass, lighting and handles clear. Office branding is restrained. Workwear uses small chest marks and preserves reflective zones.

Site operational and safety messages remain distinct from the brand. Final safety signs use the project’s required symbols and instructions. Manufacturer guidance governs decoration of protective equipment.

07 / Photography & editorial

Show measurements, skilled hands, material finishing, teamwork, inspection and completed spaces. Use natural light and plausible Saudi environments. Concept photography in this case study is AI-assisted and does not assert actual client projects.

Social designs are illustrative. Do not present concept spaces, completion posts or satisfaction messages as verified project evidence or testimonials.
