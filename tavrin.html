02 / PROJECT OVERVIEWTHE FICTIONAL BRIEF

Make time for what matters.

TAVRIN is a fictional Riyadh fintech studio. PULSE 01 imagines a watch that brings time, spending snapshots and a user-controlled payment confirmation into one considered interaction. This launch introduces that idea to GCC professionals, technology editors and retail partners.

03 / THE CHALLENGE

How do you make financial technology feel clear and human in a room full of screens? The identity needs to explain the product quickly, guide guests confidently and stay readable at event scale.

04 / THE IDEA

The interval: a moment to choose.

Three offset bars give a simple sequence a visible rhythm: pause, confirm, continue. They work as a small device-side mark, a six-metre arrival graphic and a clear stage landmark. Time becomes space for a decision.

12 / PROJECT SUMMARY

A complete identity that connects financial technology, guest movement and event production: from a compact pass to a large stage, with controlled typography and a consistent print-to-screen system.

Self-Initiated Concept Project

TAVRIN, its product and the event are fictional. No client commission, attendance, commercial result or delivered event is claimed. Mockups are AI-assisted concept visualizations; SVGs provide the original flat artwork.

Materials and dimensions communicate design intent. These are concept masters, not fabrication-approved files. A venue survey, engineered supports, fire-rating checks, font preparation, print proofs and supplier sign-off would precede production.
